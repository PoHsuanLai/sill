Changes made: each emoji's animated WebP (512 px, fetched from fonts.gstatic.com as the source
site links it) was decoded, its loop rotated to begin at the rest pose, resampled every 80 ms,
scaled to 128 px and 256 px frames, quantised to a 256-colour palette and packed into one
sprite-sheet PNG per size by tools/emoji (design/25-EMOJI.md). manifest.json records each
source URL and its sha256. No endorsement by Google is implied.
